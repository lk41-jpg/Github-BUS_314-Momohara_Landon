## Why Ratio Analysis Matters

Raw financial statements — the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement — provide the foundation of financial reporting, but they do not, on their own, tell us *how well* a company is managing its resources relative to its size, peers, or historical performance.

Ratio analysis transforms these raw figures into comparable, meaningful signals. It enables us to answer questions that dollar figures alone cannot: Is Apple generating adequate returns for the capital it employs? Is it carrying too much leverage relative to its earnings power? Is it converting sales into cash efficiently? Are liquidity buffers sufficient to weather near-term obligations?

Without this layer of analysis, management decisions risk being made on incomplete information. Ratio analysis surfaces the story *behind* the numbers — and that is precisely the insight senior leadership needs to make confident strategic and capital allocation decisions.

---

## Six Ratio Categories

This project will analyze Apple's financial performance across six standard categories:

| Category | What It Measures |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Performance** | How effectively the company generates returns relative to equity, assets, and invested capital |
| **Profitability** | Margin structure across gross, operating, and net income — how much of each revenue dollar is retained |
| **Efficiency** | How productively the company manages its assets, inventory, and receivables to drive revenue |
| **Leverage** | The degree to which debt finances the business and the company's capacity to service that debt |
| **Liquidity** | The ability to meet short-term obligations using current assets and operating cash flows |
| **Du Pont Analysis** | A decomposition of return on equity into its component drivers — margin, asset turnover, and financial leverage |

## Data Sources

Financial data will be drawn from the following primary sources:

- **SEC EDGAR** — Apple's most recent Annual Report (Form 10-K) for audited income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement figures
- **Apple Investor Relations** (investor.apple.com) — supplemental segment data and earnings press releases
- **Yahoo Finance / Bloomberg** — cross-validation of key line items and historical trend data

All ratios will be calculated from fiscal year-end figures, with prior-year comparisons included where data is available.
